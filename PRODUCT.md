# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Guests seated at a cafe table who scan a 3Diner QR code, browse dishes, optionally inspect them in 3D or AR, and place an order from their phone without creating an account.

## Product Purpose

3Diner helps cafe guests decide what to order, assemble a shared table order, and send it to the cafe with clear payment and status feedback. Success means a guest can browse, adjust quantities, confirm the table and notes, choose QRIS or cashier payment, and understand what happens next without needing staff assistance.

## Brand Personality

Warm, confident, tactile. The product should feel like a thoughtful digital extension of a good cafe menu: inviting during browsing, precise during checkout, and reassuring when payment is pending.

## Anti-references

Avoid delivery-app urgency, cramped quantity controls, generic SaaS dashboards, repeated card grids, and checkout screens that hide the total or payment consequence until the last action. Avoid playful decoration that competes with dish content or makes a seated guest feel rushed.

## Design Principles

1. Show the next decision clearly: each screen should make the next useful action obvious.
2. Preserve confidence: totals, item quantities, table identity, and payment route stay visible at the moment of commitment.
3. Keep the table order in the guest's control: editing is immediate, reversible, and does not erase the rest of the cart.
4. Use spatial depth with restraint: food and cafe identity lead, interface chrome supports them.
5. Treat payment as a state transition, not a surprise: explain what happens after QRIS or cashier selection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, keep touch targets at least 44px, provide item-specific accessible labels for quantity actions, preserve keyboard focus on validation and errors, and support reduced motion. The customer flow must work at 375px wide without horizontal scrolling.
