# 3Diner Brand Identity

## Brand Essence

3Diner adalah platform smart menu 3D/AR untuk cafe dan restoran. Produk ini membantu tamu melihat hidangan dalam bentuk 3D sebelum memesan, sekaligus memberi owner cafe dashboard operasional untuk mengelola menu, pesanan, analytics, penjualan, jadwal, promo, dan inventory.

## Name

3Diner

## Tagline

Lihat Sebelum Memesan

## Category

Interactive 3D/AR smart menu and cafe operations dashboard.

## Product Promise

Membuat pengalaman memilih makanan lebih yakin, visual, dan interaktif, sambil memberi pemilik cafe alat operasional yang rapi untuk mengelola menu dan performa bisnis.

## Target Users

- Cafe owners who want a modern menu experience without building their own technology stack.
- Restaurant operators who need simple menu, order, inventory, and revenue control.
- Staff who need quick order visibility and operational clarity.
- Customers who scan a QR menu and want to see the dish before ordering.

## Brand Personality

- Modern but warm.
- Technical but approachable.
- Premium but practical.
- Food-forward, visual, and spatial.
- Confident without sounding corporate.
- Playful in the 3D/AR experience, disciplined in the dashboard.

## Visual Personality

3Diner should feel like a boutique F&B technology product: deep navy structure, warm orange action, clear hierarchy, and tactile product surfaces. The food and the 3D model are the emotional center. The admin dashboard should feel operational, calm, and high-signal.

## Logo Meaning

The logo mark is a 3D cube/box with fork and spoon forming the "3D" idea. The full logo combines the mark with the 3Diner wordmark.

## Voice And Tone

Indonesian:

- Clear, friendly, direct.
- Use cafe/operator language, not abstract SaaS jargon.
- Prefer "Pesanan masuk", "Stok kritis", "Lihat model 3D", "Omzet hari ini", "Atur menu".
- Avoid generic lines like "Tingkatkan pengalaman pelanggan dengan solusi revolusioner".

English:

- Clear and product-led.
- Prefer "Today overview", "Critical stock", "3D model views", "Order conversion".
- Avoid hype-heavy language.

## What 3Diner Should Never Feel Like

- Generic AI SaaS dashboard.
- Purple/blue gradient tech template.
- Food delivery clone.
- Overly playful admin tool.
- Empty marketing page pretending to be a product.
- Neon gaming interface.
- Beige artisan cafe template.
- Corporate ERP with no food personality.

## Competitive Reference

The product competes with 4D Smart Menu style capabilities:

- QR smart menu.
- 3D and AR dish preview.
- Menu management.
- Order flow.
- Admin analytics.
- AI-assisted 3D/model workflows.

3Diner should feel more focused, warmer, and easier for Indonesian cafe owners.
